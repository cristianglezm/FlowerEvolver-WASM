Fine-tune the classifier on my flowers

Nudges the species prototypes towards the flowers you have, then re-classifies them. It does not add species, it spreads your flowers over the existing ones. Each epoch is one pass over your flowers; a few are enough. Flowers made before this was added are prepared once first, which is the slow part.

Split this species

Trains a small second map on the flowers of this species alone, to tell apart flowers the main map puts together. It needs about 3 flowers per sub-species and it does not change the main classifier.
